01 / About

I keep medical
devices compliant.

I'm a senior Regulatory Affairs and Quality Assurance consultant for medical devices, working with manufacturers in Europe, the US and beyond. I focus on quality systems teams can run day to day and keep audit-ready, not binders that live on a shelf.

I act as outsourced PRRC under Article 15 of the MDR for manufacturers and importers who need regulatory coverage without a full-time hire. I also work as CISO, connecting information security to the demands of connected medical products.

Earlier in my career I co-founded a medical device startup. We built it, certified it, and it didn't make it. I learned more from that than from most of what went to plan.
